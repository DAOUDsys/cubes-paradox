## Full description

Cubes Paradox is a puzzle game about boxes that hold whole rooms.

Push boxes onto their spots — simple enough. But the colored boxes are different: each one holds a complete room. Walk into a box and you are inside it. Push a box into another box. Carry boxes in, push them back out, and stack rooms inside rooms inside rooms. Some rooms even contain themselves: walk out of one and you walk straight back in.

**14 worlds, each with its own idea**
After the first world, everything is built on rooms inside rooms, often two or three levels deep:

• Workshop — the art of pushing
• Doorways — a box is a door: push it, or walk right in
• Storage — park a box inside a room, then take it back out
• Moving Day — pack a room, then move the whole room
• Unpacking — get boxes out; walking out pushes what is outside
• Rooms in Rooms, The Goal Room, Deep Dive — two and three levels deep
• Mirror Hall — a room that holds itself
• Holes, Cracked Floor, Colors, Control Room, One-Way Doors — new rules, all about rooms in rooms

**Every level earns its place**
Every level was checked by a solver: it can always be solved, every box in it is needed, and the harder ones have no square to spare and usually only one way through. The first two worlds are gentle, the next four step up, and the rest are hard — the last three levels of every world are hard challenges.

**A new hard puzzle every day**
The daily puzzle is always challenge-level. Keep a streak going.

**Stars, achievements, and hints in two steps**
Solve in the fewest moves for three stars. Unlock 12 achievements. Stuck? First buy the idea in one sentence; if you still need it, buy your next moves. Unlimited undo — or just double-tap the board.

**Made for phones**
• Swipe to move, one hand is enough
• Smooth zoom into and out of boxes; a hero who blinks, gasps and hops
• Sound effects made on the fly; vibration
• Close the app mid-level and come back right where you were
• Works fully offline — no ads, no accounts, no data collected
• 7 languages: English, Arabic (full right-to-left layout), Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Turkish

Settings can now be opened from inside a level, beside the moves, so you can change the sound and go right back to your puzzle.

## What's new (1.4.0)
The first 6 worlds are free and one purchase unlocks the other 8. Rooms in Rooms is harder. Solving a level now pays by stars: 20, 15 or 10 points. The daily puzzle offers only the idea hint.

Now in 7 languages: Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Turkish join English and Arabic. Pick yours in Settings. The background music is gone; the sound effects stay. 1.2.1 brought the swaying sprout and a layout that fits small screens and split screen.
