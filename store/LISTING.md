## Full description

Cubes Paradox is a puzzle game about boxes that hold whole rooms.

Push boxes onto their spots — simple enough. But the colored boxes are different: each one holds a complete room. Walk into a box and you are inside it. Push a box into another box. Carry boxes in, push them back out, and stack rooms inside rooms inside rooms. Some rooms even contain themselves: walk out of one and you walk straight back in.

**350 puzzles across 18 worlds**
Every world is built around one idea and has its own colors. The first worlds teach the basics; later worlds bring new rules and mix them together:

• Workshop — the art of pushing
• Greenhouse — step inside the boxes
• Warehouse — pack and unpack rooms
• The Well — rooms in rooms in rooms
• Mirror Hall — a room that holds itself
• Ice Cave — everything slides until it hits something
• Power Station — hold every plate down to open the gates
• Factory — one-way arrow tiles
• …and ten more worlds that combine these ideas, all the way to The Paradox.

**Fair, never impossible**
Every single level has been checked by a solver, so every puzzle can be solved. Difficulty climbs steadily inside each world, and the last three levels of every world are optional challenges for when you want something harder.

**Stuck? You are never stuck for long**
Solve levels to earn points. Spend them on a hint that shows your next moves — even if you have wandered off the right path — or skip a level and come back later. Unlimited undo and instant restart are always free.

**Made for phones**
• Swipe to move, one hand is enough
• Smooth animations that zoom into and out of boxes
• Works fully offline — no internet needed
• No ads, no accounts, no data collected
• English and Arabic (full right-to-left layout)
