Cubes Paradox is a puzzle game about boxes that hold whole rooms.

Push boxes onto their spots: simple enough. But the colored boxes are different, because each one holds a complete room. Walk into a box and you are inside it. Push a box into another box. Carry boxes in, push them back out, and stack rooms inside rooms inside rooms. Some rooms even contain themselves: walk out of one and you walk straight back in.

14 WORLDS, EACH WITH ITS OWN IDEA
• Workshop: the art of pushing
• Doorways: a box is a door, so push it or walk right in
• Storage: park a box inside a room, then take it back out
• Moving Day: pack a room, then move the whole room
• Unpacking: get boxes out; walking out pushes what is outside
• Rooms in Rooms, The Goal Room, Deep Dive: two and three levels deep
• Mirror Hall: a room that holds itself
• Holes, Cracked Floor, Colors, Control Room, One-Way Doors: new rules, always about rooms in rooms

EVERY LEVEL EARNS ITS PLACE
193 levels, all checked by a solver: each one can be solved and every box in it is needed. The first worlds are gentle, then it gets harder, and the last three levels of each world are hard challenges.

A NEW HARD PUZZLE EVERY DAY
Solve the daily puzzle and keep your streak going.

STARS, ACHIEVEMENTS AND HINTS
Solve in the fewest moves for three stars and unlock 12 achievements. Stuck? Buy the idea in one sentence and, if you still need it, your next moves. Undo as much as you like, or just double-tap the board.

MADE FOR PHONES
• Swipe to move, one hand is enough
• Works on small screens and in split screen
• Close the game mid-level and come back right where you were
• Sound effects and vibration
• Works fully offline, with no ads, no accounts and no data collected
• English, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Turkish
